How it works

Six steps, start to finish

1

Assess

We visit your land and check soil, water, access and what the market nearby wants.

2

Plan

We pick the crop, the growing method and the budget, and share a written plan.

3

Agree

A clear written agreement: who does what, who pays what, how revenue is shared, how either side exits.

4

Grow

Our team runs the field: inputs, irrigation, labour, crop protection, monitoring.

5

Sell

We grade, pack and sell to our buyer network, and keep the records.

6

Settle

You get the sale records and your share of the revenue, on a fixed timeline.

Revenue share

You get the bigger share, and you can see the working

When the crop is sold, the agreed costs are taken out first. What is left is the distributable revenue. You receive the larger share of it and we receive the smaller share, in the proportion written into your agreement.

We share the same outcome you do. If the farm does badly, we earn less. That is the point of the structure.

What we do not promise

  • We do not promise a fixed income. Farming income depends on yield and on price, and both move.
  • We do not promise a particular yield. We plan for a range and we tell you the range.
  • We do not promise that a subsidy will be approved. We prepare the file properly and follow it up. The department decides.
  • We do not promise that one crop suits every piece of land. If your land is not suitable, we will say so.
  • We do not take any deduction that is not written in your agreement.

We are probably not the right fit if…

  • Your holding is smaller than CONFIRM acres.
  • There is no assured water source and no way to create one.
  • The title is disputed or the revenue records are unclear.
  • You need income in the first few months. Crops take time.
  • You are not able to commit for at least one full crop cycle.
